Resident Email – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

What the app does

Resident Email is a program that runs on your own computer. When you sign in with Google, it asks for permission to access your Gmail account so it can read and organize your email on your behalf.

Data we access

With your permission, the app accesses your Gmail messages and mailbox settings. This access is used only to provide the features you see in the app.

Where your data goes

Your email data is processed on your computer. We do not run servers that receive, store, or sell your email content. We do not share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sign-in tokens

Google sign-in tokens are stored on your computer so you do not have to sign in every time. They are not sent to us.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Resident Email's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app from your computer removes the locally stored tokens and data.
